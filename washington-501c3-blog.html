Announcement

Officially Recognized as a
Washington Nonprofit

AI4DeafBlind has been issued non-profit certification from Washington State — so every dollar can go further for DeafBlind students.

September 22, 2026 · Seattle, Washington, USA · 1 min read

Seattle, Washington, USA — September 22, 2026 — AI4DeafBlind has just been issued a non-profit certification from Washington State. Our new, official, non-profit status allows us to better serve our DeafBlind community as we move forwardmwith every dollar being stretched even further to be able to facilitate learning and communication with Braillify. We are thankful for our board members and our government leaders who have been instrumental in making this happen!
