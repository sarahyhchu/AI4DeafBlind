The Challenge: Connection, engagement, and autonomy for DeafBlind students

The world for the DeafBlind is often artificially constrained by a lack of accessible tools, leaving education entirely dependent on the rare availability of tactile interpreters.

Our Mission

We refuse to let technology leave this community behind. Driven by a call to invent on behalf of DeafBlind individuals, our team built Braillify—a breakthrough ecosystem powered by three custom, lightweight AI models. Operating entirely offline on affordable hardware, Braillify dismantles the barriers to education and autonomy through three core innovations:

  1. Real-Time Translation: Instantly converts a teacher’s spoken lessons into physical Braille.
  2. Two-Way Voice: Allows students to type on QWERTY or Perkins keyboards, instantly generating spoken speech to communicate back.
  3. Braille Literacy: Empowers students to independently scan and read books, magazines, and printed materials.

The First Milestone: Helen Keller Indonesia

Innovation requires a proving ground. We are currently partnering with Helen Keller Indonesia in Yogyakarta to deploy Braillify where it is needed most. Your support directly funds the manufacturing and deployment of these devices, proving that affordable, AI-driven independence is not a distant dream—it is a reality we can deliver to every student today.
